# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

RED SNAPPER is a window-snapping app for the Mac. It's made by one person, Cesar Peralta, and it's built to know as little about you as possible.

## The short version

**RED SNAPPER doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.** There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## What RED SNAPPER can see

To move windows, RED SNAPPER needs to know where they are: each window's position, size and which app it belongs to. It uses this only on your Mac, at the moment you snap a window. It never reads what's inside your windows, never saves it, and never sends it anywhere.

Your settings (shortcuts, window gap, excluded apps) are stored on your Mac only.

## Network connections

- **RED SNAPPER from the Mac App Store** never connects to the internet. Updates come through the App Store.
- **RED SNAPPER downloaded from GitHub** checks for updates now and then by downloading a small file (`appcast.xml`) from GitHub. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address and the app's version. RED SNAPPER sends nothing else. You can turn automatic update checks off in Settings.

## Permissions

- **App Store version:** asks to control **Shortcuts Events**, so it can run its "RED SNAPPER Snap" shortcut, which moves the front window.
- **GitHub version:** asks for **Accessibility** access, which is the macOS permission that lets an app move other apps' windows.

You can revoke either at any time in *System Settings → Privacy & Security*.

## Children

RED SNAPPER doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
